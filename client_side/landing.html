Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

Harry Potter, an orphan raised by his cruel aunt and uncle, discovers on his 11th birthday that he’s a wizard. He’s whisked away to Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where he makes friends like Hermione Granger and Ron Weasley and learns about magic, Quidditch, and the wizarding world. Harry soon uncovers a dark mystery: someone is trying to steal the Philosopher’s Stone, a magical object that grants immortality and unlimited wealth. With courage, cleverness, and the help of his friends, Harry faces challenges, outsmarts danger, and protects the Stone from falling into the hands of the evil wizard Voldemort. The story is a thrilling tale of friendship, bravery, and the beginning of Harry’s destiny in the battle between good and evil.
